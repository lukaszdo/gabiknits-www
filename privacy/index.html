Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: Gabi Knits has no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics. Your PDF patterns, projects and row numbers stay on your device and never reach us. The app connects to the Internet only to handle a purchase and to check that you have access. Being offline never stops you from reading your chart.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is ŁUKASZ DOMAŃSKI IT ONE STUDIO, ul. Marii Kuncewiczowej 11, 62-050 Mosina, Poland, tax ID (NIP) PL7321956817, the developer of Gabi Knits (“we”).
For anything about privacy, contact: contact@gabiknits.app.

2. What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device. We cannot see it and have no access to it:

  • copies of the PDF patterns you open,
  • projects: names, marked charts and keys, current rows, working time,
  • settings, such as the app language.

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include your project list, progress and settings in the backup on your Google account. Copies of PDF patterns are excluded from it. Google manages that backup on your account; we have no access to it.

Deleting a project in the app removes it from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all its data.

3. Purchases and access checks

Gabi Knits is paid after a free trial. You buy it in Google Play or the App Store, and we use the Adapty service to verify purchases.

The store (Google Play, App Store)

The store handles payment, card details, your e-mail address and billing under its own terms: Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy. The store does not give us your name, e-mail address or card details. Sales reports show us only the order number, product, price, date and country of purchase.

You cancel a subscription in the store’s subscription settings, not in the app and not with us.

Adapty

Adapty checks on our behalf whether a purchase or trial is valid and stores its status. The app does not create an account: Adapty assigns a random profile ID that is not linked to your name or e-mail address. Adapty receives:

  • the random Adapty profile ID,
  • purchase data: product, transaction ID and purchase token from the store, date, price and currency, trial and renewal status,
  • technical data: device model, system and app version, language and store country.

We configure Adapty not to store your IP address or advertising ID. An IP address is technically visible with every Internet connection, but it is not saved in the profile.

Adapty Tech Inc. (USA) processes this data as our processor under a data processing agreement. The data is stored in the USA (Adapty’s providers: Amazon Web Services, OVH US, Cloudflare). Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. More: Adapty privacy policy for app users.

4. Crash reports

The app itself does not send error reports. If sharing diagnostics with Google or Apple is turned on in your device, the store may pass us aggregated, non-identifying reports on crashes and app performance. You control this in your device’s system settings.

5. When you write to us

When you write to contact@gabiknits.app, we process your e-mail address and message in order to reply. The message is forwarded by Cloudflare (Email Routing) to a Gmail mailbox (Google). We keep correspondence for up to two years after the last message, unless it is needed longer to resolve a matter.

6. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or scripts; the font is loaded from the same server. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which may log visitors’ IP addresses in server logs for security. More: GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable form, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to contact@gabiknits.app.

Because there are no accounts, we do not know who you are. To help us find your data, please include the order number from the store’s purchase receipt (in Google Play it starts with “GPA.”).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live. Our lead authority is the Polish President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
